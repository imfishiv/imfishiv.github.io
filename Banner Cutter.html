Limits are what each platform accepts for profile images as of September 2026: Bluesky around 1 MB for banners and avatars, X headers 2 MB to be safe (some sources say 5 MB), YouTube banners 6 MB with a 1546 × 423 safe area, Discord around 10 MB. Discord profile banners need Nitro, and server banners need Boost Level 2 (Level 3 for animated). Over a limit, the platform either recompresses your file or rejects it. The avatar circles on banners are approximate.
